# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. We pledge
to make interaction in project spaces harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code and behavior.
- Assume good intent, but address concrete impact and evidence.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Do not publish another person's private information, credentials, data, or
  security details.
- Follow the local-first and data-safety boundaries in `AGENTS.md`,
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private data or working exploit
details are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately through the
contact method on the repository maintainer's profile. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.
